ShieldVPN Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This policy describes the ShieldVPN Android app (com.shieldvpn.android). ShieldVPN is an OpenVPN-based VPN client: it contains an OpenVPN engine, but it does not operate VPN servers. You connect through third-party VPN servers run by independent operators, using an OpenVPN profile (.ovpn) you supply. This policy is written to be accurate, not reassuring: where a limit exists, it says so.

What the app collects

Nothing. ShieldVPN has no user accounts, no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, and no backend server operated by us. The app sends us no data of any kind.

What is stored on your device

  • The OpenVPN profile you supply is not stored. It is processed on your device and held in memory only while you connect; it is never written to disk, and the app does not send it anywhere.
  • App settings: theme choice, onboarding completion, and your acceptance of the in-app VPN disclosure.

Uninstalling the app deletes all of the above. There is no built-in server list and no automatic server discovery in the app.

VPN connection processing

When you connect, this device's IP traffic is routed through an encrypted OpenVPN tunnel to the server named in the profile you supply. Encryption happens on your device with the OpenVPN 3 engine; decryption happens on that server. Only connect while using a profile you trust.

What the VPN server operator can technically observe

Whoever operates the server you connect to can technically observe:

  • your device's public IP address;
  • connection and disconnection times and session durations;
  • the amount of data transferred;
  • the destination IP addresses of your connections;
  • your DNS queries, if the profile routes DNS through that server.

ShieldVPN does not operate the servers you connect to; they are run by independent third-party operators (unless you run your own server, in which case you are the operator). We make no "no-logs" promise about third-party servers, because we cannot verify one. Any logging on a server is that operator's practice, outside our control, outside our ability to audit, and outside this policy.

If you connect to a VPN Gate volunteer server

VPN Gate is a volunteer-run relay service, and its relays are operated by unknown third parties. VPN Gate documents its logging in its published anti-abuse policy: connection logs are kept for at least three months, including your source IP address, the hostnames, IP addresses and ports you connect to, connection times, and the volume of data transferred. VPN Gate states that these logs may be disclosed to police, prosecutors, lawyers or courts on request. Each volunteer relay also keeps packet-header logs for at least two weeks. ShieldVPN does not run these relays, cannot control or audit their logging, and cannot guarantee their availability or behaviour.

Public IP check

While connected, the app queries https://api.ipify.org to display the public IP address seen through the tunnel. That request leaves via the VPN server; the app sends no identifier with it. If the check fails, the app shows no IP rather than guessing.

Data retention

We retain nothing, because we receive nothing. Retention by a VPN gateway operator is governed by that operator.

Your rights

Because no personal data is collected by the app, there is nothing held about you to access, correct, export or delete on our side. No profiles are stored; local app settings are removed when you uninstall the app.

No anonymity guarantee

A VPN does not guarantee complete anonymity. It encrypts traffic between your device and the VPN server and changes the IP address websites see. Websites and apps you sign in to can still identify you.

Changes

Material changes to this policy will be reflected in this document with a new date, in the app and in the copy published with the app's store listing.
